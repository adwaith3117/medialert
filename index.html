How MediAlert Works

Four simple steps towards reliable medication compliance and clinical clarity.

01

Register & Add Regimens

Create your account to receive a unique Patient ID ('MED-PT-0001'). Enter prescribed medicines and dosage timings.

02

30–Minute Alerts & Alarms

Receive proactive 30-minute advance warnings followed by dosage-time alarms and repeated reminder cycles until taken.

03

Synchronized Health Calendar

Every doctor visit, medical report, and prescription automatically synchronizes into a color-coded interactive calendar.

04

Clinical Doctor Oversight

Authorized doctors view your complete medical timeline in read-only mode, eliminating forgotten paper slips.

OUR MISSION

Bridging the gap in medication adherence

More than 50% of chronic disease complications stem from missed medications and delayed clinical follow-ups. MediAlert was engineered from the ground up to solve this challenge through intelligent, persistent reminders and centralized clinical records.

With MediAlert, patients never wonder when their next dose is due, caregivers enjoy peace of mind, and physicians inspect complete adherence logs with zero data ambiguity.
